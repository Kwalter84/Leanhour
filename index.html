About

An owner's-side advocate — for your tech stack, not your build.

Leanhour is based in Strongsville, Ohio, and grew out of two decades running commercial construction and facilities programs — qualifying contractors, running competitive bids, executing AIA contracts, and keeping anywhere from 6 to 16 projects moving at once without losing sight of budget or schedule.

That background is the lens every engagement runs through: the same rigor used to vet a general contractor gets applied to vetting a software vendor. We've built and coached teams of project managers, run multi-site rollout programs across hundreds of locations nationwide, and led compliance programs touching nearly a thousand facilities — so we know the difference between a tool that looks good in a demo and one that survives a live jobsite.

Every engagement runs through the same lens, whether you're a specialty sub buying your first estimating software or a developer connecting five job sites' worth of data into one view.
